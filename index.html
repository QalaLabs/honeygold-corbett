Wildlife Sightings Planner

When Should You Plan Your Corbett Journey?

Jim Corbett's climate changes dramatically across the year, and understanding animal migration and waterhole visits is the key to extraordinary safari sightings.

Founded in 1936 as Hailey National Park, this 520 sq km sanctuary features river valleys, sal woodlands, and chaur grasslands that host 260+ Royal Bengal Tigers, Asian Elephants, leopards, and over 600 species of resident and migratory birds.

98% Sighting Season

March to June offers exceptional predator sightings near riverbeds.

600+ Bird Species

November to February brings Siberian and Himalayan migrants.

Seasonality & Weather Guide

Corbett Wildlife Calendar

Winter (Nov – Feb) | 5°C to 20°C

All core zones (Dhikala, Bijrani, Durga Devi) open. Crisp misty mornings, roaring campfires, and peak birdwatching.

Summer (Mar – June) | 22°C to 38°C

Shrinking forest streams draw tigers, elephants, and leopards to Ramganga banks. Unmatched photo opportunities.

Monsoon (July – Oct) | 24°C to 32°C

Lush emerald greenery and swollen waterfalls. Jhirna and Dhela buffer zones stay open for tranquil monsoon ecotourism.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key things to know before planning your Jim Corbett national park trip.

Core safari zone permits (especially Dhikala and Bijrani) are released 45 days in advance for Indian citizens and 90 days for foreign nationals on the Uttarakhand Forest Department portal. Due to strict vehicle caps (typically 30 vehicles per shift), permits sell out within minutes. We advise contacting Honeygold India at least 30 to 60 days before your travel dates.

Every traveler must carry the exact original Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) used during the permit reservation. Forest gate rangers verify IDs stringently before allowing vehicles inside the reserve. Foreign nationals must carry their original passport and valid Indian visa.

Dhikala Zone offers the highest tiger and elephant congregation along the Ramganga riverbanks and vast grasslands. Bijrani Zone is equally celebrated for dense tiger sightings amidst sal trees. For round-the-year safaris, Jhirna and Dhela provide consistent predator and sloth bear sightings.

Corbett's gateway city is Ramnagar (Uttarakhand). It is approximately 250 km from New Delhi (5 to 6 hours drive via NH9 / Moradabad). The Ranikhet Express connects Delhi directly to Ramnagar Railway Station overnight. Pantnagar Airport (80 km) and Dehradun Jolly Grant Airport (160 km) are the closest air connections. Honeygold arranges private luxury cab pickups from Delhi and Pantnagar.
