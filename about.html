Wildlife Conservation & Heritage

The Cradle of Indian Wildlife: Hailey to Corbett

Established in 1936 as Hailey National Park, Jim Corbett is India's first and oldest national park. It was later rechristened in tribute to legendary author and conservationist Jim Corbett, who dedicated his life to preserving Uttarakhand's ecological equilibrium.

Spanning over 520 square kilometers of hills, riverine belts of the Ramganga and Kosi, marshy depressions, and dense Sal forests, Corbett was chosen as the historic inauguration ground of Project Tiger in 1973. Today it shelters one of the world's highest densities of wild Royal Bengal Tigers, alongside Asian elephants, leopards, and over 600 species of migratory and resident birds.
